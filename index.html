Three markets

Same core job, three countries

Uganda holds the long work-order and roaming record. DRC is procedures, IN, and packet-core change. Sierra Leone is APN, UGW cleanup, messaging, and the transmission that lands on the core.

UG · Uganda

Uganda

The deepest file is here: a numbered work-order series on the MSC, MGW, USN, UGW, HSS, and u2000, plus roaming tests and weekly KPI packs.

  • MSC and MGW licenses, MSOFTX3000 patches, codec and board work, and BSC/RNC migration onto a new MSS.
  • SCCP migration and national plus international trunk moves, including SIP toward Sierra Leone.
  • USN and UGW upgrades, APN autocorrect, GCDR changes, NTP, BFD, and the cutover to new Infoblox DNS servers.
  • HSS license and OMU sync work, and LTE operator-determined barring for outbound roaming.
  • Inbound and outbound roaming tests with IR.21 sets across partner networks.
  • u2000 extracts for voice, packet core, HLR, VPD, interconnect, and call setup.

CD · DRC

DR Congo

Procedures and node work on the Huawei core and Alepo IN, including a packet-core DPI move and MSC data-fill growth.

  • Huawei MML and Alepo command procedures used on the live nodes.
  • CUG data-unit recharge flows on the IN.
  • UGW DPI shift for Katanga.
  • MSC CNACLR table growth and license updates on the nodes.
  • Design notes, cell creation support, and the spare-parts and work-order trail beside the core.

SL · Sierra Leone

Sierra Leone

APN build, gateway cleanup, messaging interconnect, and the E1/STM edge that the core depends on.

  • 3G and 4G APN creation, including designs that bypass Alepo.
  • PDP session deactivation and UGW cleanup.
  • Fraud-number blocking, and USSD short-code creation.
  • E1, TDM, and new STM fiber where transmission meets the core.
  • International SIP trunks and PCCW A2P.
  • HSS SPID work and a QCI change from 7 to 8.

What I run

Core domains

These are the systems in the daily record. The same names show up in the work orders, the procedure folders, and the screenshots.

01

MSC

Circuit-switched call control on Huawei MSOFTX3000. Batch MML, office directions, call-setup investigation, patches, licenses, and MSS rehomes.

02

MGW

Media gateway beside the MSC. Codec sets and channel counts, VPU and board recovery, announcement tones, and H.248 toward the server.

03

M3UA

SIGTRAN between the MSC and EIR, IN, and VAS. Link faults, unavailable routes, and paused SCCP subsystems, worked from u2000 alarms.

04

HLR / HSS

Subscriber and APN procedures, SPID, license loads, OMU file-sync alarms, and LTE barring for outbound roamers.

05

SMSC

SMS MO/MT toward interconnect partners, A2P (including PCCW in Sierra Leone), and number-level messaging faults on the core.

06

SGSN

Huawei USN. Software and license windows, probe configuration, BFD session binding, subcards and disks, NTP, and detach of inactive users.

07

GGSN / UGW

Gateway upgrades and licenses, PDP context cleanup, DPI relocation, APN autocorrect, and GCDR version changes.

08

APN

Private and public APN design for 3G and 4G, including profiles applied with Alepo bypassed, checked on HLR and packet core together.

09

IN / Alepo

Alepo procedures, CUG data recharge, bypass designs, and CAMEL destination numbers written into MSC CDRs.

10

Roaming

Inbound and outbound partner tests, IR.21 document sets, roaming configuration, and LTE controls on the home core.

11

u2000

Alarm browsing, user-defined counters, PS-node migration onto the NMS, and the KPI workbooks the core team actually uses.
